# Horse complete_v2 failure attribution (diagnosis)

Date: 2026-10-06
Branch: upstream-sync
Harness: `horse_fec_v2_sim complete_v2` (strict + usable + first-cause)

## Complete definitions

- **Strict:** LSF CRC ready (opening or fragments), tag match on voice,
  signature ready if signed, voice FN starts at 0, all 300 voice payloads
  verified, EOT seen, no mid-TX coast unlock. (Does not replace older
  study `ok` metric.)
- **Usable:** LSF CRC ready, tag match, signature if required, and at
  least 95 % of voice frames verified (ceil 285/300), with mean start FN
  among usable trials.

Tag check is the known 4-byte payload tag in the modem campaign (not
libsodium). Signature check is dedicated FN frames or fragment rebuild.

## Like-with-like vs commit 390a4f0e (pre-118cb0c2)

Same seeds, noise, miss_limit=4, real demod, `setSkipDcBlock(true)`.

Study-era `complete_v2` (index-aligned M17 chunks, v1 sync words in
firmware at that commit):

| noise | seed | encrypt | sign | both |
|------:|------|--------:|-----:|-----:|
| 10000 | 1 | 144 | 152 | 144 |
| 10000 | 11000 | 140 | 145 | 140 |
| 8000 | 1 | 195 | 195 | 195 |

That reproduces the study's **144/200** (seed 1, encrypt, noise 10000).

Current v2 attrib **strict** at the same point is **93/200**; **usable**
is **168/200**. Difference from 144: (1) counting is FN/tag/EOT-based
not index-aligned; (2) v2 sync words and 3-frame opening + fragments;
(3) strict requires EOT and FN0. The old campaign's 144 is closest in
spirit to usable-without-95%-rule "all 300 voice after LSF", not to
today's strict.

noise 10000: 0/1000 frames. False locks in 10 s open noise: 0.

replacement recommended for correlation reasons; alt has weaker peaks.

## Gate arithmetic

Hard voice FER at noise 10000 on locked single frames was ~1e-4 in the
FEC study. For 300 independent frames,
`(1-p)^300 >= 198/200 = 0.99` needs `p <= ~3.3e-5`. Measured effective
loss under complete TX (timing + hard decisions) is higher: strict
~0.46 success => effective p ~0.0026 per frame.

**198/200 strict at noise 10000 with hard decisions is not achievable**
from the measured per-stage rates (LSF almost free; voice dominates).

Proposed gates for owner approval (do not change without approval):

1. Keep 198/200 as an **aspirational** soft-decision / future-FEC target.
2. Operational hard-decision gate: **strict >= 180/200 at noise 8000**
   (measured 179--186) and **usable >= 165/200 at noise 10000**
   (measured 159--173), or
3. **usable >= 198/200 at noise 8000** (measured 195--198; nearly met).
